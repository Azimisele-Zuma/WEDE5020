OUR STORY

Brief History

Azi's Bakery was started in 2019 by Gcobisa Zuma a single mother from tembisa. She began baking at home using heartfelt recipes she learnt from her grandmother. She sold cakes and scones to her neighbours and took small orders for eventsx. As more people discovered her baking, demand grew, allowing her to buy better equipment and open a small bakery. Today the bakery is known for affordable,homemade treats and stands asd a symbol of hard work and determination

Vision

To become the most loved local bakery,known for creativity,quality and uniting individuals through sweet treats

Mission

Our goal is to provide, delicious, high quality baked goods that bring joy to every customer while creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere for our community. From freshly baked bread to crafted cakes and pasteries, we are dedicated to making every moment a little sweeter
