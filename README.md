# WEDE5020
WEB DEVELOPMENT PROJECT
## Project Title
Azi's Bakery
## About Website
Azi"s bakery website is a warm, community-focused e-commerce and brand showcase for an artisanal bakery founded in 2019 by Gcobiza Zuma, a single mother from Tembisa. Built on foundation of family recipes passed down from her grandmother.. The website bridges the gap between a heartfeltlocal success story and an oline shop.

The website serves 3 main purposes:

Sharing and inspiring brand narrative:
Through dedicated history,vision and mission sections, the website introduces visitors to the bakery's roots,highlighting its evolution from small home-kitchen operations to a well known local business.

Showcasing the product menu:

The platform organises the bakery's diverse, affordable and homemade offerings into four distinct categories for easy browsing:Artisan Bread, Cakes and cookies, Sweet pasteries and beverages

Driving Customer engagemnet:
Equipped with features like a shopping cart, a general search tool and enquiries portal and a dedicate contact page. The entire website functions as a digital portal for the community to order treats, ask questions or book services for events


Overall the website acts as a warm,welcoming digital home that reflects the brand's goal.


## PROPOSAL

1.)	ORGANISATION OVERVIEW:
•	NAME: AZI’S BAKERY

•	BRIEF HISTORY: Azi’s Bakery was started founded in 2019 by Gcobisa Zuma a single mother from Tembisa. She began baking at home using heartfelt recipes she learnt from her grandmother. She sold cakes and scones to her neighbours and took small orders for events. As more people discovered her baking, demand grew, allowing her to buy better equipment and open a small bakery. Today the bakery is known for affordable, homemade treats and stands as a symbol of hard work and determination

•	MISSION: To provide fresh, high-quality baked goods using accessible ingredients, while delivering great customer service and creating a welcoming atmosphere for the community.
VISION: To become the most loved local bakery, known for creativity, quality and uniting individuals through sweet treats.

•	TARGET AUDIENCE: Individuals that want to taste sweet treats made with nothing but love.
